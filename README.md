Abandoned But Not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars
An interactive web-based storytelling and educational platform developed for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge by Team Cosmic Eclipse.

🚀 About the Project
Since the 1960s, NASA and global space agencies have left hardware across the solar system—from rovers and scientific instruments on the Moon and Mars to probes traveling deep into space. "Abandoned But Not Forgotten" is designed to bring these historical pieces of equipment back into the spotlight. The platform combines storytelling, interactive space exploration, mapping, and quizzes to educate school-age space enthusiasts and the general public about space exploration history and the science behind these missions.

🌐 Live Demo
https://abandonedbutnotforgottenchallenge.netlify.app

✨ Key Features
Interactive Space Explorer: Visualize and explore discarded equipment and spacecraft locations.

Mission Stories & Cinema Mode: Engaging narratives detailing the history and purpose of abandoned hardware on the Moon and Mars.

Interactive Maps & Inspector Tools: Detailed inspection and geospatial context of landing sites and equipment.

Educational Quizzes & Timelines: Interactive components to test knowledge and track space mission milestones.

Multilingual & Responsive UI: Clean design optimized for seamless user experience across devices.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6)

3D & Graphics / Assets: Three.js (Vendor modules)

Data Handling: GeoJSON, Custom JSON datasets for mission facts and objects
