Paused: how to play

Keyboard

  • WASD or arrow keys: move
  • Space: low-gravity jump
  • Shift: move faster
  • E: interact
  • Esc: pause or close a panel
  • Drag with the mouse to turn the camera; scroll to zoom

Touch

  • Left thumb: joystick
  • Drag on the scene to turn the camera
  • Tap the glowing button to interact
  • Jump button at the bottom right

Walk to a portal in the Space Hub to visit the Moon, Mars or Deep Space. Walk up to a glowing beam to examine a spacecraft and earn its badge. Missions are grouped by world for learning; they did not all happen in one place. The 3D layout is not to scale.

Reset your progress?

This removes all discovery badges and your Mission Log progress on this device. Your astronaut stays. This cannot be undone.
