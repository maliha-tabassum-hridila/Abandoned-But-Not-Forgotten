Across the Moon, Mars, and beyond, NASA has left machines behind. Some completed their missions. Some lost contact. Some remain silent on distant worlds. But each one changed what we know about space.

02 / The Timeline

Six decades of hardware, left in place

1960s

NASA begins landing hardware on other worlds — the Surveyor program tests the Moon for Apollo.

1970s

Crewed Lunar Modules land six times. Pioneer 10 and 11, then Voyager 1 and 2, head for deep space.

1990s

Sojourner becomes the first wheeled rover to explore another planet.

2000s

Spirit, Opportunity, and Phoenix expand the search for water on Mars.

2010s

InSight listens to the inside of Mars. Voyager 1 and 2 both cross into interstellar space.

2020s

The Voyagers keep transmitting, decades past their five-year plan.
